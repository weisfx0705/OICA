01 / The journey

From city trains
to ocean waves.

Start at Zuoying High Speed Rail (HSR) Station in Kaohsiung. The 9189 Kenting Express is the simplest main connection to the Kenting area.

Step 1Zuoying HSR StationFind the Kenting Express bus counter
➜
Step 2Bus 9189Reserve/check schedule before leaving
➜
Step 3Kenting areaTransfer to a local bus or prearranged pickup

Indicative fare: around NT$401 one way or NT$650 for a promotional round-trip ticket. Tickets, eligibility, stops, and departure times must be checked on the official Taiwan Tourist Shuttle website ↗.

Important: Not every campsite is within safe walking distance of the express bus. Some require a second local bus or an arranged pickup. Do not plan long roadside walks on coastal highways, especially after sunset.

03 / Make memories

A simple 2-day escape.

Day one / Coastbound

Meet the sea.

  1. Morning: Meet as a group at Zuoying HSR Station; board the 9189 bus.
  2. Midday: Transfer or get your pre-booked pickup; check in and inspect your gear.
  3. Afternoon: Enjoy a permitted beach viewpoint or designated coastal walk.
  4. Evening: Eat together, enjoy the sunset, and return to camp before dark.

Day two / Head home

One last ocean view.

  1. Morning: Breakfast, photos and a short coastal walk.
  2. Late morning: Pack up, check nothing was left behind and check out.
  3. Afternoon: Take your planned local transfer and 9189 bus back to Zuoying.
  4. Arrival: Message your university contact to confirm everyone returned safely.

This is an adaptable outline, not a confirmed bus schedule. Do not swim at beaches where swimming is prohibited; only enter the sea when authorized and safe. Camping and fires are prohibited outside designated areas in Kenting National Park.

05 / Before you book

Ask first.
Book with confidence.

Copy this short English message to the campsite. For faster replies, ask a Chinese-speaking classmate or university contact to translate if needed.

Hello! We are a group of international students from Thailand planning a 2-day, 1-night camping trip to Kenting. We will travel from Zuoying HSR Station by public bus and carry only regular backpacks.\n\nCould you please confirm:\n1. Can we rent a complete tent, sleeping mat, sleeping bag, pillow and light? Can your staff set up the tent?\n2. What is the total cost for 4 people sharing one tent, including all equipment?\n3. How do we reach your campsite from the nearest bus stop? Do you offer pickup?\n4. Do you have hot showers, drinking water and charging points?\n5. Can we make a group reservation? What is your cancellation policy for strong winds or heavy rain?\n\nThank you very much!

Booking advice: Confirm the exact date, group size, arrival time, total price, payment method and refund rules in writing. Do not assume a listed tent price includes a sleeping bag or shower access.

06 / A note from your university

Great adventures
begin with care.

We hope you make beautiful memories along Taiwan's southern coast. Your wellbeing is always more important than the itinerary.

🧭 Safety comes firstTravel together, avoid isolated roads at night, and check the weather, waves and strong-wind alerts.
🧥 Stay warm and dryEven tropical Kenting can feel cold and windy at night. Bring warm layers, rain protection and dry clothes.
💊 Bring essential medicationCarry your own necessary medicines, personal health supplies and enough drinking water.
📱 Keep in touch with the universityShare your route, campsite, travel companions and emergency contact. Check in regularly, especially upon arrival and return.
🆘 Emergency assistance in TaiwanPolice: 110 · Ambulance / Fire: 119. Tell responders your location and ask for translation help if needed.
